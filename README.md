# Animated Food App Splash Screen (Flutter)

Welcome to the **Animated Food App Splash Screen** project! This Flutter-based app introduces an elegant, interactive, and smooth splash screen animation that adds a modern touch to any food-related application. The splash screen features a rotating plate in the center of the screen that gradually grows in size, creating an engaging and dynamic visual effect before transitioning to the home screen of the app.

This project serves as a perfect starting point for any Flutter developer who wants to implement a visually appealing animated splash screen in their food app or any other similar project.

## Features

- **Animated Splash Screen**: A smooth, visually appealing splash screen animation with a rotating plate that grows in size.
- **Platform Compatibility**: Fully compatible with both Android and iOS, ensuring a consistent user experience across all platforms.
- **Easy Navigation**: Once the animation completes, the app seamlessly navigates to the main home screen of the food app.
- **Customizable Animation**: Easily adjust the animation speed, size, and duration to match the design of your app.
- **Lightweight & Optimized**: Built with efficiency in mind, the animation is optimized for both performance and battery life.

### How It Works:
- The splash screen starts with a small rotating plate in the center.
- As the animation progresses, the plate increases in size and continues to rotate smoothly.
- Once the animation ends, the app navigates to the home screen (or your app's main content screen).
